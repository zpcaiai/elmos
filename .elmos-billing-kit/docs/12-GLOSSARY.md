# Glossary

- **Execution credit**: customer-facing unit used to pay for Elmos execution; internally stored as integer micro-credits.
- **Paid credit**: credit backed by customer payment.
- **Promotional credit**: granted credit with separate expiry and cash-equivalence rules.
- **Price book**: versioned customer-facing price configuration.
- **Vendor rate book**: versioned internal provider cost configuration.
- **Metering**: capture and normalize resource usage.
- **Rating**: apply a rate version to usage to produce cost or charge.
- **Estimate**: prediction of future resource, cost and runtime.
- **Quote**: customer offer binding scope, price version, expiry and cap.
- **Authorization/hold**: permission and reserved funds before execution.
- **Capture**: convert authorized amount into settled consumption.
- **Release**: return unused authorization.
- **Cap**: maximum accepted customer charge for a defined scope.
- **Fixed price**: predetermined project price independent of actual cost, subject to scope and acceptance.
- **Change order**: approved change to scope, price, schedule or acceptance.
- **BYOK**: customer supplies model-provider credentials; Elmos still charges applicable platform resources.
- **Machine wall-clock ETA**: elapsed autonomous system execution time, excluding human development effort.
- **Suspense**: temporary account/work queue for unresolved reconciliation differences.
- **Idempotency**: repeated identical command produces one business effect and returns the stored result.
- **Outbox/inbox**: reliable event publication and consumption pattern for at-least-once delivery.
