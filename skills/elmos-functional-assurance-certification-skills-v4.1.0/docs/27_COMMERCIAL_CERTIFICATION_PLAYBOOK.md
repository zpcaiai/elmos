# Commercial Certification Playbook

A procurement-grade release produces: architecture and data-flow maps; product/model cards; security and privacy impact assessments; quality/SLO/DR evidence; SBOM/AIBOM/VEX/provenance; support/incident/PSIRT procedures; certificate scope and verification instructions; waiver, expiry and residual-risk registers.

Answers to customer questionnaires are generated only from current authorized evidence. Unknown, legal, contractual or organizational claims are routed to qualified humans rather than invented.
