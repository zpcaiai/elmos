# AI Governance and Compliance Profiles

Profiles translate NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OWASP LLM/Agentic/Agentic-Skills baselines and regional rules into candidate controls, implementation tasks and evidence obligations.

The compiler does not decide legal applicability. It records jurisdictions, effective dates, unresolved legal decisions, required reviewers, technical controls, retained evidence and deployment restrictions. A legal-review-required item remains blocked until an authorized decision is bound to the exact RevisionSet.
