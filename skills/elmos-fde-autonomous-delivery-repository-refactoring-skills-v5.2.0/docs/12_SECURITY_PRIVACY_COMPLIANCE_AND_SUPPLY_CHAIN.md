# 12 — Security, Privacy, Compliance and Supply Chain

## Platform controls

Tenant isolation, RBAC/ABAC, OIDC/SAML/SCIM, environment-owned authority, leases, verified context, egress policy, secret brokerage, encryption/CMK, residency, retention/deletion/legal hold, audit export, WORM evidence options, rate/quotas and break-glass.

## Software supply chain

Pin source/tool/dependency/image identities; generate SBOM; scan vulnerabilities/licenses; sign artifacts; capture build/transformation provenance; verify at admission. Release-time values remain explicit placeholders until the target organization supplies and verifies them.

## Compliance boundary

Elmos can collect and organize evidence, map controls and identify gaps. It must not claim legal interpretation, accreditation, regulator approval or conformity solely from automated checks.
